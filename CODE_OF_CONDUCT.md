# Code of conduct

WarpCad adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**
as its code of conduct, for everyone taking part in this project's issues, pull requests,
discussions and other spaces.

In short: be welcoming and patient, assume good faith, criticise ideas rather than people, and
accept that the maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributors that don't meet that
standard.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer privately at
**conduct@warpcad.com**. Reports are handled confidentially, and the enforcement
guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 apply.
