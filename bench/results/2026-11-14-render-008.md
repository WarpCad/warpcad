# render-008: orbit frame time before and after the theme bridge

- Machine: Apple Mac (darwin 27.2), headless Chromium via Playwright (software rasterizer; GPU cost is not represented), 1440x900.
- Build: production (`npm run build`, served by `vite preview`). **Before** is a `git worktree` of `3998bbc` (HEAD); **after** is the working tree with the render-008 diff.
- Scene: two 40 mm cuboids (`bench/orbit-frame-time.mjs`), a real left-button drag circling the canvas for 5 s, first 0.6 s discarded.
- Method: `node bench/orbit-frame-time.mjs --url <preview> --runs 3 --label <name>`, two rounds each, before and after interleaved. 900 frames per row.

| build | round | p50 ms | p95 ms | p99 ms | worst ms | frames over 16.7 ms |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| before | 1 | 16.7 | 17.6 | 18.4 | 18.7 | 365 / 900 |
| after | 1 | 16.7 | 17.8 | 18.5 | 18.8 | 336 / 900 |
| before | 2 | 16.7 | 18.0 | 18.5 | 18.7 | 349 / 900 |
| after | 2 | 16.7 | 17.9 | 18.4 | 18.7 | 334 / 900 |

The frame rate is vsync-bound at 60 Hz in both builds, so the distribution is the display's cadence plus about 1 ms of jitter. No change within run-to-run spread (p95 17.6-18.0 before, 17.8-17.9 after). The orbit makes zero `getComputedStyle` calls (`e2e/viewport-scaffold.e2e.js`, all nine projects).
