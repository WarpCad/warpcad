- The skill's default is to cut the mating part with a copy of the inserted part grown by 2 × clearance. That only works if the inserted part can still be seen as a single body. Once `Pin` is a part inside a group, it is not addressable on its own.
- Restricting a group operand with `pieces: "Base"` did not keep the other parts. The tool treated the group as consumed and returned a single body with no parts.
