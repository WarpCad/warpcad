**Machine**: Apple M4 Pro, macOS 27.2.1, Node v24.22.2. **Cura**: `CuraEngine` 5.13.0 from UltiMaker Cura, stock Ultimaker

**Input.** A generated, closed, outward-wound, lumpy UV sphere written as a binary STL in inches (`NLON` x `NLAT` grid: 60 x 42,
160 x 102, 300 x 162, 480 x 252), read with `parseStl` and `weldMesh`, built with `solidFromMesh` (Units inch), a 25 x 25 x 75 mm
cuboid subtracted at the body's bounding-box centre, `exportBodies(..., 'stl')`. The first row is a 12-triangle 40 x 20 x 10 mm
box with a 12 mm cutter. "Open" is an edge not used by exactly two triangles, in opposite directions, with corners quantised at
1e-5 mm.

## Why `overlapping faces` remains

Cura melds vertices within about 30 micrometres and flags two faces on one edge at an angle of exactly 0. In the 32k file the
cutter's flat bottom face has two 16.6 mm edges 17 micrometres apart with a thin triangle pair between them, which Cura folds
onto one edge although the file is closed there at 1e-5 mm. A global 30 micrometre fuse is wrong in principle (the uncut 240k
scan has 7 micrometre pole rings Cura accepts). Follow-ups: `io-032` (the sub-30-micrometre flat-face cleanup) and `io-033`
(the one 1.4 micrometre four-face edge left at 96k).
